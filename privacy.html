Privacy Policy for ParasocialCRM

Effective Date: January 13, 2026

1. Introduction

ParasocialCRM ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

ParasocialCRM uses YouTube API Services. By using our application, you are agreeing to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Cookies and Device Storage Information

We access and store information on your device. Specifically, we utilize local storage and cookies to securely store authentication tokens (OAuth 2.0 tokens) required to maintain your connection to the YouTube API services. This allows the application to function without requiring you to log in repeatedly during a session.

3. Data We Collect

  • YouTube User Information: We access your public YouTube profile information (such as channel name and avatar) via the YouTube API.
  • Live Chat Messages: We process live chat messages in real-time to enable interactive features like polls, trivia, and to track viewer engagement.
  • Engagement Metrics: We analyze chat participation to identify loyal fans and assist creators in their content strategy.

4. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected information solely to display live chat messages, calculate poll results, and identify frequent interactors ("loyal fans"). We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Data Retention

We store authorization tokens and user data only for as long as is necessary to provide the service consistent with your consent. We do not retain data for fixed arbitrary periods (e.g., 12 months) without active usage. If you disconnect the application or cease usage, the associated tokens are removed from our active storage.

  • Users can revoke ParasocialCRM's access to their data via the Google Security Settings page.
